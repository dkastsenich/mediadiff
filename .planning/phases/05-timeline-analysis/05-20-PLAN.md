<objective>
Finish Gap 4. Feed every MPEG-TS decode-timestamp consumer the container's own DTS, taken from 05-15's PES-header seam, and correct the declared sets that enshrined libavformat's inferred tie.

Output: the DTS-source fields, the orchestrator post-pass and union rule, dts_monotonic evidence and skip rule, the doc update, and corrected declared sets.
</objective>
