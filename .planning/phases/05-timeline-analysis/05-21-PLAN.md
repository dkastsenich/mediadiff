- Design a checkpoint mapping that is piecewise across timestamp discontinuities, and prove or disprove it in a scratch harness.
- It must make doc 04 section 5's step recipe (two segments concatenated with a 100 ms audio trim at the join) yield two stable plateaus and a step time.

- 05-10-SUMMARY.md proved that a single whole-file affine checkpoint map cannot produce two plateaus.
- A splice is either smeared into linear-drift or collapsed into a transient irregular.
- The remaining question is whether a piecewise map is sound, and what it must assume about a timestamp discontinuity. The human needs that answer, with evidence, before product code changes.

Output: a calibrated harness, recipe builders, and `05-STEP-DESIGN.md` carrying the recorded human Decision.

<flagged_assumptions>
**Edge-probe items assigned to this plan (unresolved in the fallback report, surfaced here, never dropped):**
- **TIME-07 / boundary** ("What happens exactly at each min/max/threshold, and one step either side?")
  - The research must state, for its recommended design, the behavior at exactly three things:
    1. The step threshold (`kDriftStepResidualMultiple` * `kDriftEpsilonMs` = 6 ms). The shipped rule is "strictly greater".
    2. The plateau flatness bound (2 ms of each side's own mean).
    3. The discontinuity-detection threshold it introduces.
  - Each is pinned with a harness case one ms either side.
  - The implementation spec must carry these as unit-test vectors for 05-22.
- **TIME-07 / precision** ("Where can precision loss, overflow, or rounding/tie-breaking occur, and what is the exact contract?")
  - The research must name every division and rounding its mapping introduces.
  - Example: the segment-proportional target, t_a = seg_start_a + delta * span_a / span_v. Its rounding is to the nearest tick with ties away from zero, matching the shipped nearest_tick, or it names a different rule and justifies it.
  - It must name the overflow bound for each product, which must fit Int128Accum/ExactInt.
  - The harness uses Fraction arithmetic, so it measures the rounding error the integer implementation will incur.

**A1: timestamp-only step detection is ambiguous by construction.**
- A 100 ms audio timestamp gap is produced identically by two edits:
  - trimming 100 ms of content while keeping the original timestamps (sync preserved, a dropout);
  - shifting timestamps by 100 ms over contiguous content (sync stepped).
- The packet counts, durations and timestamps are the same in both cases. timeline_drift_step.mp4 is built exactly this way: a 3.9 s sine with PTS +4410 at 44.1 kHz from audio packet 95 on.
- A design can reach `step` only by assuming the second reading. It will then report `step` for a dropout-style trim.
- The research quantifies this. The human decides with it in view.

**A2: residual MP4-to-TS drift.**
- 05-14 Task 2 found that the MP4-to-TS pairs' av_drift fail comes from libavformat's estimated TS audio stream duration (3877 ms against 4023 ms) being used as the checkpoint span. It does not come from priming.
- The same pair's av_drift.pattern flips to irregular.
- The span source is part of the checkpoint mapping. The research therefore also reports what each candidate does on those pairs, and whether using observed packet extents instead of estimated declared durations removes a false positive.
- Any resulting change is presented to the human at the decision, not adopted silently.
</flagged_assumptions>
