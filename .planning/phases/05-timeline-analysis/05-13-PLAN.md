<objective>

and its first real instruction-count measurement, transcribe both into the committed golden files, and
confirm the designated leg green with every leg-only gate actually run.

provisional ledger. Phase 4 hit exactly this and closed it in its own 04-20 and 04-21 plans; this plan is
that same two-step, applied to Phase 5's fixtures plus the new performance baseline.
Output: a `CORPUS_DIGEST.txt` with no workstation-derived line, an empty-on-purpose provisional ledger, a
real `PERF_BASELINE.txt`, and a confirmed-green designated leg.
