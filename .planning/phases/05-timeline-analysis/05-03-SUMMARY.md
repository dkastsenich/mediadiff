## Decisions Made

- **`conforms_to_grid`'s tolerance-boundary arithmetic uses `checked_sub`/`checked_add`, not raw `+`/`-`.** An adversarial timestamp near `INT64_MIN`/`INT64_MAX` could make `actual_delta - tolerance` or `actual_delta + tolerance` themselves overflow (undefined behavior in plain arithmetic); routing through the checked helpers means this degrades to `insufficient_data` like every other overflow path in the file, honoring T-05-09's threat-register mitigation exactly.
- **`ideal_interval_num`/`ideal_interval_den` are stored unreduced** (`span_ticks`/`interval_count` directly, never GCD-simplified) — matching the plan's own "never pre-divided" instruction, since Phase 5's D-06 grid-relative histogram bins need the exact same fraction cadence's own consumers compute against.
- **The rate formula's reciprocal relationship is explicit**: `tb.den*interval_count/(tb.num*span_ticks)` is algebraically `1/(average_interval * tb)`, the reciprocal of the average tick interval scaled by the timebase — chosen over restructuring `Cadence` to expose a pre-computed rate field, since PROBE-10's own "derive, don't bake" rule says the analyzer computes its own statistic from the shared primitive's raw counts.
