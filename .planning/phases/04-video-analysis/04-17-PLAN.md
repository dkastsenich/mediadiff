    - If the diagnostic does NOT fire with the suppression removed, remove the block entirely,

      liability.

      region that silences the warning: the single `emit_*` function body GCC named, not the file,
      and not the whole anonymous namespace.

    optimisation level, with which compiler version, and that the suppression is now bracketed
    rather than open-ended. A comment claiming a scope the pragma no longer has is the same class of
    defect as the ones this phase is closing.

    Do not touch anything else in these six files. Plans 04-14 and 04-15 have already landed their
    behavioural changes to `stream_params.cpp` and `interlace.cpp`; a behavioural diff appearing
    here means something went wrong.
