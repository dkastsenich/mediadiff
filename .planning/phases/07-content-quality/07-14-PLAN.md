encodes the proof streams (including the libx264/libx265 ones the LGPL corpus cannot), every leg

gate make a class-1 claim impossible without committed evidence. It lands report-only; 07-15
transcribes the first CI run and promotes proven decoders.

Purpose: CONTENT-01's cross-machine hash claim (D-09) cannot be true for any video codec until this
proof exists. Output: `scripts/gen_video_proof.sh`, the proof test and its golden support, the
`video-proof-streams` CI job and artifact handoff, and the ledger file.
</objective>
