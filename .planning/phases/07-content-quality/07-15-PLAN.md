<flagged_assumptions>
**A31 — One five-leg run is sufficient evidence to promote.** D-09 asks for a committed
cross-architecture proof, not a count of runs. One run with all five legs identical is taken as
enough because gate mode re-checks every class-1 row on every later run: a decoder that later
diverges fails CI loudly and is demoted with its evidence, never silently trusted.

**A32 — Regenerating under `TZ=UTC taskset -c 0-3` predicts the designated leg's fixture bytes**
(project memory and the resolved test-898 session). Used only to make the first run's designated
digest assertion likely to pass; the predicted lines stay provisional and are replaced by what the
run actually printed. If the prediction misses, the designated leg stops at the assertion and Task 1
iterates (see its instructions).

**A33 — Promotion is keyed by the decoder name each leg reports, not by the stream.** If two legs
decode the same proof stream with different decoders (for example a different AV1 decoder being
chosen first on one build), neither decoder is promoted from that stream; the row records the
designated leg's decoder and the others' names are written into the evidence.
</flagged_assumptions>
