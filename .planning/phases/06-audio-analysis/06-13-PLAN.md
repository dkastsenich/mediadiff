arm64 target was available to test it, so the claim is `[ASSUMED]`. Both outcomes are planned for
below. If the probe shows a difference, `mp3` and `mp2` — and the already-shipped `aac_fixed` and
`ac3_fixed` — are demoted to class 2 on the affected legs with the measurement recorded, which
degrades cross-architecture hashes to `skipped:hash_incomparable` rather than to a fabricated fail.

rather than a failure of the design.

as a promise of a single green run.
