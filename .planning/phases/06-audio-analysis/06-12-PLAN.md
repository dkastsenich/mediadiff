<objective>
`PERF-04`'s harness: the audio sweep of the 10-minute reference stereo AAC file measured and printed
as wall clock, gated by an instruction-count ratchet against a committed baseline, wired into the
designated CI leg.

Purpose: Phase 5 learned this lesson expensively. An absolute wall-clock assertion on a shared runner
is a flaky test, and Phase 4's own measurement of the same class of workload swung 33-53% between
runs. The ratchet against a committed retired-instruction-count baseline is what actually gates, and
the absolute number is recorded rather than asserted. Reusing `scripts/measure_timeline_perf.sh`'s
proven three-mode shape — rather than writing a second measurement idiom — keeps both harnesses'
failure modes identical and readable.
Output: `scripts/measure_audio_perf.sh`, its bench target, the first committed baseline line, and the
CI step that runs it on the designated leg.
</objective>
