<objective>
Close WR-02 from 06-REVIEW.md (VERIFICATION.md gap 3). The review's correction confirmed it as P0-class, though today's corpus cannot reach it.

Today `sampling_state` is the constant "full". A stream the consecutive-error limit cut short therefore hashes a prefix and compares it as a real content FAIL against a full decode. That is the fabricated-verdict class TRUST-02 exists to prevent. This plan:
- makes the stop observable,
- degrades the hash comparison to skipped:hash_incomparable,
- makes every level check skip with the reason, instead of reporting a number measured over part of the stream.

It also establishes the one stop-reason vocabulary and doc table. 06-15 (CR-02) and 06-16 (CR-03) append to them.

Purpose: an early-stopped decode must never produce a verdict (D-09 and TRUST-02).
Output: the truncation fields and latch, the sampling_state vocabulary, the comparator rule, level-check skips, tests and docs.
</objective>

<flagged_assumptions>
**A1: "truncated" is a conservative label.** The latch fires when the 65th consecutive failure arrives. It does not check whether packets remained. A stream whose final 65 packets are all corrupt is labelled truncated even though the demuxer had nothing left. That only degrades the stream to a skip, which is the safe direction, never to a verdict.

**A2: meta.decode_errors is unchanged.** It still reports the count up to the stop, 65 for a limit-truncated stream, with first_error_reason. Its value is honest: it is the number of errors seen. The stop is surfaced by sample_hash and the four level checks. WR-06's meta.decode_errors precondition gap stays deferred.

**A3: the drain after a latch is unchanged.** finalize() still drains the decoder. Frames buffered from packets decoded before the stop still reach consume_frame. That is harmless because the hash is labelled truncated and the level readouts are discarded. 06-15 adds a consume_frame guard for its own latch.
</flagged_assumptions>
