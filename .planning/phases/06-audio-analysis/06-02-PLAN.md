<objective>
Every fixture this phase's eight analyzer plans assert against, built once, in one place, with the
generators and text goldens they need — the hand-written HE-AAC signaling pair the pinned toolchain
cannot encode, the lossless loudness/true-peak/silence fixtures with their committed `ffmpeg -af
ebur128` reference, and the layout, sample-format, codec and priming fixtures.

Purpose: the pins ship no HE-AAC encoder anywhere (Linux/macOS martin-riedl builds carry no
`libfdk_aac` despite `--enable-nonfree`; the Windows pin is `win64-lgpl`), and the native `aac`,
`ac3` and `eac3` encoders produce different bytes at different SIMD levels. Building every fixture in
one plan means ONE designated-leg digest transcription round trip at the end of the phase instead of
eight, and it lets each analyzer plan assert against fixtures that already exist rather than
discovering a recipe gap mid-implementation.
Output: `tools/gen_he_aac.py` with a decode-round-trip `--selftest`, roughly two dozen new corpus
recipes, and the committed loudness reference text.
</objective>
