<objective>
ROADMAP SC1's user-visible half: `mediadiff inspect` renders a complete audio section, and the whole
corpus is swept to prove no fixture compared against its own clean partner reports anything.

Purpose: every preceding plan in this phase proved a measurement is CORRECT. This one proves it is
VISIBLE — SC1 is written in terms of what a user sees — and it proves the thing this project exists
for: that adding nine audio checks to a corpus where most fixtures carry AAC audio did not create a
single false positive on an unchanged pair. That sweep is the phase's own answer to "false positives
are P0", and it is much cheaper to run here, once, than to discover during verification.
Output: the rendered audio section with its goldens, a registry-enumerated rendering assertion, and a
corpus-wide clean sweep.

    its SBR signaling mode, the sample rate, the sample format, the bit depth, the channel count and
    the canonical layout — ROADMAP SC1's own list — followed by the decode-derived rows (priming,
