<objective>
`audio.loudness.integrated` and `audio.loudness.true_peak` — EBU R128 loudness and true peak computed
by libebur128 inside the shared decode sweep, matching the committed `ffmpeg -af ebur128` reference
within ±0.1 LU, with the asymmetric −1.0 dBTP headroom rule that fails on an upward crossing.

Purpose: loudness and true peak are the two audio measurements that catch a silent re-level — an
enhancement stage quietly changing the mix — which no parameter check can see. The asymmetric peak
rule matches the real risk: headroom loss causes clipping after a downstream lossy encode, while
headroom gained does not. Getting the channel-role mapping right is the difference between a correct
number and a silently wrong one on exactly the 5.1(side) content this phase already singled out.
Output: the libebur128 sink in the shared sweep, two registered checks with their `--explain` docs
and fixture pairs, and one generic comparator escalation carrying the asymmetry.
</objective>
