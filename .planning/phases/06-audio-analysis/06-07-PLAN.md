<objective>
Close `WINDOWS.md` #32 by extending Phase 5 D-10's shared-basis rule from offsets to `av_drift`'s
checkpoint span, so a lossless MP4-to-TS remux stops reporting a 42 ms `irregular` drift on an
untouched payload.

Purpose: a false positive on a lossless remux is the P0 class this project exists to prevent, and
ROADMAP SC2 already scopes closing the `priming: unknown` gap `av_offset`/`av_drift` shipped with to
this phase. The fix consumes 06-06's resolver work: MPEG-TS declares no per-stream duration, so
libavformat ESTIMATES it from the PTS range, and that estimate includes the AAC priming and padding
samples an MP4's box duration already excludes — two spans measured on different bases, compared as
if they were the same. The rule is the one this phase keeps rediscovering: a measurement whose basis
differs between two files must compare on the basis they share, or say so.
Output: a priming-state-gated span, one generic comparator extension, and a ledger entry moved from
`waived` to `fixed` on evidence.
</objective>
