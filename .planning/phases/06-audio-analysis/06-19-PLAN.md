<objective>
Close WR-07 from 06-REVIEW.md (VERIFICATION.md gap 3).

`span_ticks_for_basis` sets `prefers_declared` from input availability: priming and padding merely supplied. When the trimmed reconstruction then fails and falls back to the container's untrimmed declared field, the measurement still reports `span_basis: "adjusted"`. That is a false claim about which basis was measured, and tol.cpp's D-16 override trusts it. The preference now follows the reconstruction outcome, which is D-16's own intent: "adjusted" means the span really is trimmed.

This plan touches av_sync.cpp, which also carries the amended SC2's two MP4-to-TS assertions. It re-runs them and the round-trip deviation test, and requires their declared sets unchanged.

Purpose: span_basis never lies about the basis a drift measurement used.
Output: the one-line semantic fix, a corrected contract comment, boundary tests, SC2 re-runs, docs and a ledger note.
</objective>
